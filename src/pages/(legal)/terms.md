## What it is

Worbik is a free daily word puzzle. Each day brings a new puzzle for every size of board, and you solve one by rotating its rows and columns until every row is a word. You can play without an account. If you sign in, you can post your scores to public leaderboards and keep a streak.

Worbik is made and run by Drake Costa, an individual. There is no company behind it. Its [source code is public](https://github.com/Saeris/worbik).

Worbik has no accounts of its own. You sign in with an account that you hold with another service, such as Bluesky, and that service's own terms apply to it. Worbik never sees your password, and can't help you recover an account or change anything in it.

## Scores

A score that you post is public. It appears on leaderboards along with your handle, and anyone can see it, link to it, or share it.

Your first solve of a puzzle is the one that counts. Scores are only accepted during a puzzle's own day, with a short grace period, and each one is checked against the game that earned it.

Leaderboards are a bit of fun, and they are not a record of anything. Scores can be corrected, removed, or lost, and a leaderboard can be reset.
